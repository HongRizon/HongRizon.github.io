USD/KRW, forecast as a range

 

Dots mark days the rate closed outside the one-day-ahead 90% range made the day before. A calibrated 90% interval should miss about one day in ten; since 2000 this one missed 10.7% of 6,703 trading days. Point, tap, or use the arrow keys to inspect a historical forecast.

Method & data

The model is the standard exchange-rate random-walk baseline without drift, with volatility estimated from an exponentially weighted average of past squared returns (λ = 0.94). Each forecast uses only information available on that date.

The 50% range caught 55.3% of days rather than half.

Red marks the upper half of the range and blue the lower, the way Korean markets color up and down moves. Data: Federal Reserve H.10 noon buying rates via datasets/exchange-rates; last observation 25 Sep 2026.

Research

I care less about fashionable model names than about identifying what carries information, what a decision truly needs, and what a computation actually uses.

01

Multimodal market forecasting

Combining price series, market microstructure, text, accounting information, and visual representations to study what information carries predictive value in financial markets.

02

Decision-sufficient learning

Studying the information and estimands a downstream decision actually requires, from point-in-time forecasting and model selection to abstention and evaluation under costs and uncertainty.

03

Function-aware machine learning

Studying models by what their computations actually do rather than by convenient proxies, using intervention, invariance, representation design, and function-preserving transformations to make models more interpretable and efficient.
