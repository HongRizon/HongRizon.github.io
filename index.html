Quantitative researcher & engineer — forecasting under uncertainty.

Markets are distributions, not points; a forecast that hides its own uncertainty is a guess wearing a suit.

I build forecasting systems for financial markets: probabilistic models that are honest about what they don’t know, and the engineering that keeps them honest in production. Currently an M.S. student in Data Science at Jeju National University and a consultant on WorldQuant BRAIN, with first-author work on multimodal forecasting for currency and crypto markets.

Based in Jeju, KR.
